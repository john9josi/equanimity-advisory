Point Reyes Incorporation Options

Point Reyes Station is not a city. Marin County is the local government. PRSVA can stay a civic forum, ask the County for a firmer seat, form a special district, or try to become a city. Each option gives a different power. Each option also takes more time, money, and volunteer work.

Scale 895 people (2020 CDP). About 570 voters. About 3.6 sq mi.
Plan Community Plan, 1976 / 2001. The County uses it. It does not bind the Supervisors.
Neighbors No West Marin city. Muir Beach and Tomales run CSDs.

Today

Coastal Comm.

Last word on many coastal permits. A city still needs a Local Coastal Program.

Marin County

Planning, sheriff, fire, septic, roads. The Board of Supervisors is the village’s city council.

PRSVA

Volunteer forum. Reviews projects. Advises. Cannot vote no on a permit.

Districts

NMWD: water. CSA 28: paramedics. Most lots use septic.

First, name the problem

Land use

Tourism, design review, housing, downtown, dark skies, parking, the Community Plan.

A CSD cannot take these powers. Only a city gets zoning. If cityhood is too heavy: update the Plan, or ask for a municipal advisory council.

Services

Wastewater, visitor bathrooms, parks, local roads, streetlights, a community hall.

A CSD or CSA can run these. That is the West Marin pattern. Tomales and Muir Beach still do not control zoning.

If the Association cares most about…

Village character

Update the Community Plan. Ask for a MAC. Price cityhood only after a fiscal study. A CSD will not become planning.

Wastewater or a hall

Study a CSD or CSA. Tomales is the closest model. Water already has NMWD.

A louder County voice

Ask the Supervisors for a municipal advisory council. Write the map and duties in the ask.

Grants and insurance

Incorporate PRSVA as a nonprofit. (c)(3) for education. (c)(4) for more lobbying. Permits do not change.

Cityhood, if you go that far

01 Map

Need 500 registered voters inside the line. Tight map may fail. Wide map hits ranchland.

02 Petition

About 25% of voters in the proposed city, or an agency resolution. File notice first.

03 Money test

LAFCO needs three years of revenues, services, and a reserve. Plus revenue neutrality with the County.

04 What stays

Coastal Commission, NMWD, County Fire, Highway 1, the Seashore. Police is likely a sheriff contract.

Briefing for the Point Reyes Station Village Association. Not a petition, fiscal analysis, or legal opinion. Charter city is a later layer on a general-law city, not a first step.

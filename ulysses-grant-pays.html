How the Grant Pays for Ulysses

The county does not buy software as “IT.” The county writes Ulysses into the grant as delivery infrastructure for that award. Then the award reimburses the cost.

Assumptions shown on this page: the NOFO allows software, contractual, or project-admin technology; the cost is incurred in the period of performance; invoices and draws keep source documents. Change the program below to see how the budget home changes.

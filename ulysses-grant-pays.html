How the Grant Pays for Ulysses

The process is the same for every grant family. The county buys one annual Ulysses contract, then allocates a share into each award that allows it. Only the budget language changes below.

County process

  1. 01

    Attach to a project

    Frame Ulysses as delivery infrastructure for the funded work — tracking, docs, draws, reports — not as a generic IT buy.

  2. 02

    Check eligibility

    Confirm the NOFO allows software, contractual, info systems, or project admin tech. If not, leave this award out of the allocation.

  3. 03

    Write the budget line

    Put an allocated share of the annual subscription (and setup if allowed) in the right category for that award.

  4. 04

    Justify in narrative

    Tie the cost to grant duties and state the allocation method. Necessary, allocable, reasonable.

  5. 05

    Buy after authority

    Execute the county SaaS contract only when award terms and purchasing rules allow. Keep quote, contract, and invoice in the file.

  6. 06

    Allocate, draw, prove

    Charge each eligible award its share. Draw with invoice + allocation worksheet. Local dollars cover any gap.

One county contract

Ulysses prices an annual SaaS contract with the county. Grants do not set the price. Finance allocates shares of that contract across awards that can reimburse — and fills the rest from local funds.
